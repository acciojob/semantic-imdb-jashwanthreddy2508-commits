Baahubali 2: The Conclusion tells the story of a tragic royal betrayal and ultimate revenge. The movie opens with the noble prince Amarendra Baahubali choosing his love for the brave princess Devasena over his right to the throne of Mahishmati. His jealous cousin, Bhallaladeva, uses this decision to plot against him, tricking the Queen Mother Sivagami into believing that Amarendra is planning a coup. Manipulated by these lies, Sivagami orders the loyal warrior Kattappa to execute Amarendra, leading to his tragic death when Kattappa stabs him in the back. Decades later, Amarendra's hidden son, Mahendra Baahubali, learns the truth about his father's murder and his mother's long imprisonment. He rallies a rebel army, attacks the kingdom, defeats the tyrant Bhallaladeva in a brutal final battle, and finally reclaims his rightful throne.
